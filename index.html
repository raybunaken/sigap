FAQ

Questions people ask

Is my CV safe?

Your CV PDF is parsed entirely inside your browser. The file never leaves your device. For analysis, the extracted text is sent securely to our server, processed by our AI engine, and never stored.

Is Skillsy free?

Yes. Core features (score, gap analysis, ATS keywords, cover letters) are free during beta. Early waitlist members get access first.

Which job boards are supported?

LinkedIn, JobStreet, Glints, Kalibrr, Tech in Asia, Kintsugi, and MyCareersFuture, with a smart fallback that works on most other job boards too.

How accurate is the score?

Scores are computed by a fixed formula over per-requirement judgments, not free-form AI guesses. Skills are matched by a synonym engine, experience is counted in years, and identical inputs always produce identical scores.
