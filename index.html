Frequently Asked Questions

Is my CV safe? Where is my data stored?

Your CV PDF is parsed entirely inside your browser; the file itself never leaves your device. For analysis, the extracted text (plus the job posting you're viewing) is sent securely to our server, processed by our AI engine, and never stored or shared.

Is Skillsy free to use?

Yes. The core features (ATS score, skill gap analysis, and keyword suggestions) are free during beta. Pro features (like AI cover letters at scale) may be paid in the future, but early waitlist members get privileged pricing.

Which job sites are supported?

LinkedIn, JobStreet, Glints, Kalibrr, Tech in Asia, Kintsugi, and MyCareersFuture, with smart fallback extraction that works on most other job boards too.

How accurate is the match score?

Skillsy combines rule-based skill matching (with synonym detection, e.g. ReactJS = React) and AI semantic matching. Transferable skills (like PHP → Python) get partial credit, so the score reflects how a recruiter would realistically assess your profile.

When does the beta launch?

Early Beta Access opens this September, with the public Chrome Web Store launch in November. Waitlist members get access first, in order of signup.
