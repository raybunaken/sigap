Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 16, 2026

The short version

Your CV file never leaves your device. We don't store your CV or analysis results on any server. We don't sell or share your personal data with anyone. No account required.

What stays on your device

  • Your CV PDF file. It is parsed (read) entirely inside your browser using local JavaScript. The file is never uploaded anywhere.
  • Your analysis history. The last 50 job analyses are stored only in the extension's local storage on your device. You can delete your CV and history anytime from inside the extension.

What is sent for analysis

When you scan a job posting, the extension sends two things to our server (skillsy.my.id): the text extracted from your CV, and the text of the job posting you are viewing. Our server forwards this to our AI provider (Groq) to generate the match score, skill gap, and suggestions. This data is processed in memory only; it is not written to any database, not stored, and not used to train models.

Waitlist emails

If you join our waitlist, we store your email address solely to send you your beta invitation and occasional product updates. You can ask us to remove it at any time.

Third-party services

  • Groq: AI processing of your CV and job posting text (data transit only, no storage).
  • Vercel: hosts our website and API. Standard, anonymized server logs may be generated.
  • Google Fonts: font files for this website.

Your control

  • Delete your CV from the extension at any time. One click, gone from your device.
  • Uninstalling the extension removes all locally stored data.
